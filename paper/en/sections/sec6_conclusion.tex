\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented PhysFormer, a physics-guided Transformer for physically consistent VPP forecasting. The framework combines an ExplicitPhysicalMapping stream, a bounded residual output mechanism, a physics-guided gating and alignment module, and curriculum-based constraint training. Together, these components are intended to reduce non-physical outputs while preserving the flexibility of data-driven residual prediction.

On the real-world 3-year VPP dataset considered in this study, PhysFormer achieves a boundary violation rate of 0.00\% and remains close in MSE to strong unconstrained Transformer baselines. The results suggest that the proposed framework offers a useful trade-off among statistical accuracy, physical consistency, and interpretable weather-response behavior. In particular, the PGCC module mainly improves alignment and interpretability, while the BPAR mechanism is responsible for the consistent non-negative output behavior observed in the experiments.

The present study is limited to one regional dataset, one prediction setting, and component-level forecasting without an explicit storage, state-of-charge, or dispatch closed loop. Moreover, the interpretability results should be understood as supervised alignment to known physical cues rather than causal discovery. Future work will extend the framework to additional datasets and forecasting horizons, and examine how physically consistent predictions can be coupled with downstream VPP scheduling and control models \cite{Ma2025,Arabzadeh2025,Du2025}.
