Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript, ``PhysFormer: A Physics-Guided Transformer for Physically Consistent Virtual Power Plant Forecasting,'' for publication as a Research Paper in \textit{Electric Power Systems Research}.

The manuscript presents an application-oriented forecasting framework for joint virtual power plant component prediction. PhysFormer combines an explicit physical mapping stream, a physics-guided gating and alignment module, a bounded residual output mechanism, and curriculum-based constraint training. The method is designed to improve physical consistency in load, photovoltaic, and wind forecasts under weather covariates while maintaining competitive predictive accuracy.

Using a real three-year virtual power plant dataset, PhysFormer achieves a boundary violation rate of 0.00\% with an MSE of 0.0128 and remains competitive with strong Transformer baselines. These results are relevant to power-system operation with renewable integration and align with the applied scope of \textit{Electric Power Systems Research}.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have read and approved the submitted version.
